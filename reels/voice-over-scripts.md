# Voice-over scripts for the Reels

Speak slowly and warmly. A Reel voice-over reads at about **2.5 words per second**, so each line below fits its scene. Leave the bell chime clear: don't talk over it.

---

## Reel 1: "Every bell" (5 Nov, 8 seconds)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | 10:59 | "10:59. One minute to the next period…" |
| 3–4 s | 11:00 + chime | *(silence, let the bell ring)* |
| 4–8 s | Every bell. Every day. | "Smart School 360. Set it once, and every bell rings on time." |

**Hindi:** "Das baj kar unsath… *(bell)* … Smart School 360. Ek baar set kijiye, har bell samay par."

## How to add it

**Easiest: send me the recording.**
1. Record each script as a voice note on your phone, in a quiet room, about 15 cm from the mic.
2. Send me the files.
3. I'll mix them into the Reels, lower the chime under your voice, and update the Buffer drafts.

**Or do it yourself in Instagram:**
1. Open the Reel in the editor.
2. Tap the audio or "Voiceover" button.
3. Record while the video plays.

**CapCut works too:** Audio → Record, then export.

Note: Buffer can't record audio, so the voice-over has to be in the video file before it goes into Buffer.
