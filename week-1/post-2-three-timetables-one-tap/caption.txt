Regular day. Exam day. Holiday or Saturday programme.

Every school runs at least three different timetables in a year, and switching between them usually means someone reprogramming the bell timer.

Smart School 360 stores three separate timetables (A0 Regular, A1 Exam, A2 Vacation or special). Program them once at the start of term. On exam morning, tap A1 and the exam timings take over. Tap A0 when exams end. Your regular schedule is never touched.

Save this for the next time exam week sneaks up on your office.
