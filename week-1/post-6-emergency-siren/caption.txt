Most schools have a fire extinguisher. Fewer have a way to alert every classroom, corridor and playground at once.

On Smart School 360 it is one button: tap the red triangle, confirm, and the siren sounds in every speaker zone. It overrides any bell or announcement and keeps sounding until a staff member stops it, so nobody can miss it.

If your school runs evacuation drills, build the siren into the drill so every teacher knows the sound.
