In a ZP primary school in Khed taluka, the bell, the prayers and the announcements used to depend on a teacher remembering.

With permission from the District Education Officer (Primary), Pune, Cortek Electro installed Smart School 360 at Z.P. Primary School, Nighoje: programmed bells, Marathi voice announcements, automatic prayers, a PA system, an emergency siren and battery backup.

The pilot report (November 2025) recorded zero missed bells and clear sound across the campus, including through power cuts. The Block Education Officer, Khed, and the DEO (Primary), Pune, both recommended expanding the system to more schools.
