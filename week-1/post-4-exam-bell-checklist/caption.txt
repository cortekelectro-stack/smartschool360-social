Prelims are close, and the bell is one thing nobody wants to think about on exam morning.

Five checks to do this week:
1. Get the board's exact timings, including the warning bell.
2. Keep the exam schedule separate from your regular one.
3. Decide which areas must stay silent.
4. Switch off music, prayers and voice announcements during paper time.
5. Test the bell during a power cut.
