# LinkedIn concepts — 9 posts, 2–20 Nov 2026
Audience for all: principals, directors, trustees, school-group management, CSR teams. ★ = chosen.

## 1 · 2 Nov · Company update (carousel)
Objective: re-open the page after a year with a confident product reveal. One message: *one screen now runs the whole school day.*
- A ★ **Apple-style product reveal.** The poster cover you liked (giant lime 360 behind the real controller), then calm white slides, one idea each. Premium and product-first.
- B Corporate announcement card: logo, "Introducing Smart School 360", plain. Credible but forgettable.
- C Timeline infographic, 2024 → 2026. Leans on history we can't fully verify.

## 2 · 4 Nov · Who rings the bell (carousel)
Objective: make the cost of manual bells felt. One message: *a manual bell is only as reliable as one person's memory.*
- A ★ **Swiss typographic shock.** A huge red-orange "11:09" with the small line "Recess was at 11:00." Then the four failure points on a strict grid, and the product as the fix.
- B Day board log with late and missed bells. Good story, but more text.
- C Photo of a teacher looking at a wall clock. Relies on stock-like imagery.

## 3 · 6 Nov · Nighoje ZP pilot (carousel)

- C Isometric illustration of a school campus with sound zones. Pretty, but it dilutes the hard number.

Copy direction for all captions: shorter (about 60–120 words), benefit-led, 1–3 emojis maximum, the free online demo CTA and 🌐 website line kept, 3–4 hashtags.
