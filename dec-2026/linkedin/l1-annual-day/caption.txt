Annual day and sports day put every part of a school's sound system to work at once: rehearsal timings, the anchor's mic, music, and bells that must not interrupt the chief guest.

Smart School 360 runs all of it from one touchscreen. A separate event timetable (A2) switches in with one tap, Silent mode pauses scheduled bells during speeches, and each zone keeps its own volume.
